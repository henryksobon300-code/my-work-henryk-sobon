# Public research portfolio — evidence and presentation standard

**Maintainer:** Henryk Soboń  
**Established:** 2 November 2026  
**Purpose:** A practical quality-control standard for public research records. This is a documentation policy, not an independent scientific validation or a claim of academic affiliation.

## Required review for each research record

1. **Exact contribution and attribution.** Identify the author's own work, other contributors' work, original upstream project, and whether a finding is a new result, an implementation, a correction, a proof for a fixed instance, or an exploratory experiment.
2. **Verifiable primary evidence.** Link directly to the relevant original issue, pull request, review, merge, proof, code, data, or archived certificate. Avoid relying only on self-authored summaries.
3. **Scope of external validation.** State exactly what an independent maintainer, reviewer, or formal checker examined. A merged software contribution is not proof of every broader research claim; a private reply is not formal peer review.
4. **Status vocabulary.** Distinguish merged/accepted contribution, externally checked result, author-side public evidence, archived/private evidence, and pending review. Give dates for snapshots and preserve historical changes.
5. **Reproducibility where warranted.** For unreviewed claims or newly published artifacts, check exact input identity, pinned version, commands, expected output, independent checker where applicable, positive/negative controls, hashes and environment. Do not automatically rerun already accepted upstream contributions solely to restate their documented review.
6. **Limits and counterexamples.** Include assumptions, the precise equivalence or mathematical model, what is *not* established, and any relevant unresolved criticism or negative test.
7. **Navigation and accessibility.** Ensure that the homepage gives a concise overview, that the project index provides the complete record, that links are descriptive and resolve, and that longer proofs remain on dedicated pages.
8. **Disclosure and privacy.** Do not publish private correspondence, proprietary engine source, patent-sensitive information, credentials, or third-party quotations without appropriate authorization.
9. **Investor-facing accuracy.** Lead with the strongest independently inspectable achievements and their practical relevance. Avoid invented affiliations, testimonials, world-first claims, unsupported numerical comparisons, and exaggerated endorsement.
10. **Maintenance.** Record the actual date and scope of each review; do not call a repository fully audited while any substantive evidence or link checks remain outstanding.

## Triage: where to spend verification effort

- **Merged upstream work:** verify merge and review links, contribution attribution, and that the public description matches the accepted scope.
- **Externally checked mathematical proof:** verify the original statement, reviewer/formalization record, precise object, and what the check establishes.
- **Open benchmark submission:** preserve exact reproducibility artifacts, versioned validator instructions, hashes, negative controls, and explicitly label organizer approval as pending.
- **Archived or private experiments:** retain a bounded public description and evidence-availability status; do not imply that private files are already independently reproducible.
