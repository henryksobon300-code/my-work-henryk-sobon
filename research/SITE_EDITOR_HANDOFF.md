## What the site must communicate

1. **Independent technical researcher** rather than an asserted academic title or institute affiliation.
2. Strongest verified contributions first: 2 directly authored and merged PRs, then 2 maintainer-adopted findings.
3. A fully browseable **evidence trail** for each case, including public discussion, independent replay, restrictions, and status.
4. Open benchmark reports are **submissions**, not officially accepted competition results.
5. MathemaSpace is an **umbrella for distinct experimental branches** and historical artifacts, not a claim of universally validated artificial intelligence or a single undisputed scientific discovery.
6. Maintain a private/public boundary for external scientific correspondence, patents and private methods.

## Information architecture for a later custom-designed site

**Home → Results index → Individual case page → Original public evidence.**

Recommended menu: About / Verified contributions / Submitted results / Research methods / External review / Contact. Each individual case should display date, source repository, reproducible test, exact assertion, reviewer / acceptance status and limitations. Add filters by status and subject only when they improve navigation.

**Do not copy entire PR/email threads to the site**: link public discussions to their canonical GitHub originals; preserve private correspondence in an access-controlled archive. Ask correspondent permission before publishing direct excerpts, names as testimonials, screenshots of private replies, or logos implying endorsement.
